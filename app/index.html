An assessment in practice

A recommendation should show its working.

Illustrative example only. Not a client case, completed transaction or assessment of a specific property.

  1. 01 / CLIENT OBJECTIVE

    Buy a plot to build a home within a defined budget and timeline.

  2. 02 / EVIDENCE REVIEWED

    Owner-provided plan, site photographs and local asking-price comparisons. These do not establish legal access or transacted values.

  3. 03 / UNRESOLVED ISSUE

    The available documents do not establish the proposed access arrangement.

  4. 04 / COMMERCIAL IMPLICATION

    Access uncertainty may affect usability, financing and resale. Specialist review is required.

  5. 05 / RECOMMENDATION — HOLD

    Obtain and review access evidence with an appropriately qualified specialist before committing.
